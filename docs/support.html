Support

Email jackwallner+p@gmail.com. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened, and you will get a real reply.

I disagree with an answer

Tell us the drill phase and what you would have hit instead, and why. Shot selection is coached opinion, and a well-argued case for a different answer is worth reading. Positions that are physically wrong (a player off court, a ball nobody could reach) are a bug, and those get fixed fast.

What system does it grade against?

Keep the ball unattackable, get to the kitchen line, and pressure the player who is not set. The Coaching System screen in the app spells it out in full. Every answer names the principle it came from.

The daily ball count

The free tier grades 15 balls per calendar day. It resets at midnight in your local time zone, so it is a daily allowance rather than a trial that runs out.

Restoring a purchase

Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops future renewals.
