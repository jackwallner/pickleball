Privacy Policy

DUPR IQ · last updated 24 August 2026

DUPR IQ does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your practice history, per-phase accuracy, streaks, daily ball count, and settings are stored on your device only. They are not uploaded anywhere, and we cannot read them.

Purchases

If you subscribe to DUPR IQ Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider. They receive an anonymous identifier and the details of the transaction so the app can tell whether your subscription is active. They do not receive your name, email address, or practice history. Apple's handling of your payment is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Analytics and tracking

There is no advertising, no third-party analytics SDK, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. The app does not request the advertising identifier.

Children

The app is a sports training aid. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
