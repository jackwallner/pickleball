Pickleball strategy

You already know how to hit it. The question is which one.

Most rec players lose points they had won, by picking the wrong shot from a good position. Third Shot puts you on a 3D court, in first person, with four places you could hit it. Then it replays your shot against the right one and names the principle behind the answer, so the next one is not a guess.

Dink rally

Nothing is written down. Read the ball against the net tape and their feet against the line.

  • Speed up, to the backhand
  • ✓ Dink, at their feet
  • Dink, cross-court kitchen
  • Lob, deep cross-court

Dink at the player who isn't set. The right opponent has not re-established at the line, so a soft ball at their feet makes them hit up, and the ball that comes back is the one you attack.

You are on the court, not reading a diagram

Every ball is the rally seen from where you are standing, and you answer by aiming at a place rather than picking a sentence. How high the ball is and whether their feet made the line are things you look at.

Points, not flashcards

Read it right and the rally continues to your next shot. Read it wrong and you lose the point, the way you would in a game. Practice starts untimed, with an optional decision timer for faster reads when you are ready.

See the miss, not just the answer

After every ball the court replays your shot, then the right one, from the same contact point. A drive at a ball below the net dies in the tape where you can watch it happen.

It finds your leak

Every third point is built around the mistake that has cost you the most, like counter-driving under pressure. Get it right and it counts as fixed; miss it again and it stays on your list.

Positions that never repeat

Court positions are generated, not shipped as the same fifty screenshots reshuffled. Every one is physically legal and has exactly one best answer.

Answers with a reason

Keep it unattackable. Get to the kitchen. Hit the player who is not set. Each answer names the principle it came from, and your mastery of each principle is tracked, so it transfers to the next rally.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app for pickleball drills?

Yes. Third Shot puts you on a first-person 3D court in a real doubles position, gives you four shots to choose from, replays your shot against the right one and names the coaching principle behind the answer. These are decision drills you do on your phone, not physical drills.

How do I decide between a third shot drop and a drive?

It depends on where all four players are, which is exactly what Third Shot trains. Each ball puts you on the court, you choose from four options, and the answer names the principle, such as keeping the ball unattackable or hitting the player who is not set.

Which parts of a pickleball point can I practice?

Serve and return, third shot, transition, dink battle, attacking a high ball and defense. Mastery is tracked per coaching principle, and every third point is built around the mistake that is costing you the most.

Do the positions repeat?

No. Court positions are generated rather than reused from a fixed question bank, and every one is physically legal with exactly one best answer.

Is Third Shot free?

The free app gives you fifteen graded balls every day with replays, focus points and principle mastery, and the count resets each calendar day. Third Shot Pro unlocks unlimited balls, your mistakes ranked, and every run kept. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

Does Third Shot change my rating?

No. Third Shot is independent and is not affiliated with any pickleball rating system or governing body, and it does not calculate, report or affect any official rating.

Does Third Shot collect my data?

No. It does not collect or transmit your personal information, and your practice history, mastery and streaks are stored on your device only.
