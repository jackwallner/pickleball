Pickleball drills

You already know how to hit it. The question is which one.

Most rec players lose points they had won, by picking the wrong shot from a good position. DUPR IQ puts you on the court, in first person, with a shot clock running and four places you could hit it. Then it names the principle behind the answer, so the next one is not a guess.

Dink rally

Nothing is written down. Read the ball against the net tape and their feet against the line.

  • Speed up, to the backhand
  • ✓ Dink, at their feet
  • Dink, cross-court kitchen
  • Lob, deep cross-court

Dink at the player who isn't set. The right opponent has not re-established at the line, so a soft ball at their feet makes them hit up, and the ball that comes back is the one you attack.

You are on the court, not reading a diagram

Every ball is the rally seen from where you are standing, and you answer by aiming at a place rather than picking a sentence. How high the ball is and whether their feet made the line are things you look at.

Points, not flashcards

Read it right and the rally continues to your next shot. Read it wrong and you lose the point, the way you would in a game. The shot clock runs the whole time, at tournament, match or relaxed pace, or off.

Positions that never repeat

Court positions are generated, not shipped as the same fifty screenshots reshuffled. Every one is physically legal and has exactly one best answer.

Answers with a reason

Keep it unattackable. Get to the kitchen. Hit the player who is not set. Each answer names the principle it came from, so it transfers to the next rally.

Graded by phase

Return of serve, third shot, transition zone, dink rally, attacking a high ball, and playing defense. Progress is tracked per phase, so you can see which one is actually costing you games.

What is free

Fifteen graded balls every day, across all six phases, and your accuracy in each of them. The counter resets each calendar day, not once for good.

DUPR IQ Pro

Unlimited graded balls, plus the history behind the numbers: every session you finish kept and scored, and the coaching principles you keep getting wrong ranked so you know what to drill next. Accuracy by phase stays free.
