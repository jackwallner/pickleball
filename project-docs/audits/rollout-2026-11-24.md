## The good moment and how often it comes

**A run that ends with half or more of its graded balls kept in play.** A run
is the core loop (up to 12 balls, points played out), and the summary shows
the score. The moment counts a playable ball as kept because the app does
(it keeps the rally and wins the point). Runs of fewer than 4 graded balls and
runs that end at the free daily limit are excluded: the first is not a result,
the second shares the screen with the Pro pitch.

How often: a free player gets 15 balls a day, so the first run of every
session is a 12-ball run that ends normally, and Pro players finish as many
runs as they like. A player who keeps half the balls (the demo history
averages 9 of 12; the advisor's playable tier exists so sound rec-level shots
keep the rally) hits the moment in every session. With the launch gate the
first ask lands in session 2, on the first good run. The old trigger reached a
typical free player on day 2 too (third finished run, counting the short run
that ends at the limit), but only once ever; the new one asks again after 30
days, inside Apple's three a year.

A player who taps "Play another run" within 3 s of the summary skips that
moment; the next good run asks.
