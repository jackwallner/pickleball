Third Shot 1.1 rebuilds the app around a first-person 3D court.

- Stand in a real doubles point and call the shot from where you are standing.
- Every ball replays your shot against the best one, on the same court.
- Sound alternatives now count: a playable shot keeps the rally going and shows in gold, with the reason the best ball was better.
- Focus points test the tempting wrong shot that has cost you the most.
- Optional decision timer at learning, game or fast pace.
