Stand on the court, read the rally, and hit the shot before the clock runs out.

DUPR IQ puts you in the point. Every ball is a real doubles position seen from where you are standing: four players, a ball hanging at an actual height beside the net tape, and the score. Nothing is written down for you. How high the ball is and whether their feet have reached the kitchen line are things you look at, the same way you would on a court.

You answer by aiming. Four rings sit on the paint where each shot would land, and you pick the place, not a sentence off a list. Then you see why the answer is the answer, named as a coaching principle you can argue with, with the geometry drawn from above if you want to see it.

Points, not flashcards. Get the read right and the rally continues to your next shot. Get it wrong and you lose the point, the way you would in a game.

There is a shot clock, because shot selection with unlimited time to think is a different skill from shot selection. Set it to tournament, match or relaxed pace, or turn it off.

Practice by phase:
- Return of serve
- Third shot drop or drive
- Transition zone
- Dink rally
- Attacking a high ball
- Playing defense

The free app gives you 15 graded balls a day, across all six phases, with your accuracy in each of them. DUPR IQ Pro removes the daily limit and adds the history behind those numbers: every session you finish kept and scored, and the coaching principles you keep getting wrong ranked so you know what to drill next.

Positions are generated, not pulled from a fixed question bank, so practice never runs out.
