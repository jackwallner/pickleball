Stand on the court, read the rally, and call the shot that wins the point.

Third Shot puts you in a real doubles point, seen in 3D from where you are standing: four players, the ball hanging at its actual height beside the net tape, and the score. Nothing is written down for you. You read the ball's height, where their feet are, and who has made it to the kitchen line, then aim where your shot should land.

Watch every shot replayed. After each ball, your shot and the right one fly from where you hit it, on the same court. Drive a ball that was below the net and you watch it die in the tape, with the coaching principle that explains why.

Points, not flashcards. Get the read right and the rally continues to your next shot: return, third shot, the walk to the kitchen, the dink rally, and the finish. A playable shot that was not the best keeps the rally alive and shows you what was better. A mistake hands them the point.

The court learns your game. Every third point is a focus point, built to test the tempting wrong shot that has cost you the most, until you stop taking it. Tell it whether you play the left or right side and positions put you there.

See how you play. Every answer is filed under a coaching principle, from keeping the ball unattackable to hitting the player who isn't set, and Your Game shows how reliably you pick each one.

Play untimed while you learn the court, or turn on the decision timer at learning, game or fast pace.

Free gives you 15 balls a day with replays and mastery for every principle. Third Shot Pro removes the daily limit, ranks the traps costing you points, and keeps every run you play.

Positions are generated, not pulled from a fixed question bank, so the court never runs out.
