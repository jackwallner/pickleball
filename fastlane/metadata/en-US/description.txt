See a real doubles rally, pick the shot, and learn the principle behind the answer.

DUPR IQ generates a new court position every ball: four players, the ball height, the score, and the phase of the point. You choose between four shots a real player would actually consider. Then you see why the answer is the answer, named as a coaching principle you can argue with.

Practice by phase:
- Return of serve
- Third shot drop or drive
- Transition zone
- Dink rally
- Attacking a high ball
- Playing defense

The free app gives you 15 graded balls a day. DUPR IQ Pro unlocks unlimited balls, every phase, and accuracy by phase so you know what to drill next.

Positions are generated, not pulled from a fixed question bank, so practice never runs out.
