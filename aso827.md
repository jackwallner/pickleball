## Executive directive

Rebrand before first public release unless written permission and trademark clearance exist for `DUPR IQ`. The product is not affiliated with Dynamic Universal Pickleball Rating, does not calculate or affect an official DUPR rating, and competes in search beside the official DUPR app. A disclaimer does not remove the acquisition and review confusion created by using the mark as the product name.

Use `Pickleball IQ` as the product brand and `Pickleball IQ: Shot Drills` as the App Store title. This preserves the useful IQ concept while accurately describing a generated shot-decision trainer.

Replace the current duplicative subtitle with `Court Position Training`. The proposed title already supplies Pickleball, Shot, and Drills. The subtitle adds Court, Position, and Training, allowing strong cross-field combinations without repeating the visible `Shot` token.

Use a 98-byte hidden field built around actual rally phases and choices. It can form `third shot drop`, `doubles court position`, `pickleball strategy`, `dink shot`, `kitchen shot`, `serve return`, and `pickleball coaching` across fields.

Replace the five-frame iPhone and iPad packages with six canonical product-evidence frames. Remove the paywall from the main carousel, make the court readable, and add the missing Court Primer frame.

- Do not imply official DUPR integration, endorsement, ratings, rankings, or analysis.
- Do not claim AI, video coaching, swing analysis, form correction, opponents, multiplayer, tournaments, leaderboards, court discovery, or a complete pickleball game.
- Do not claim that one shot is universally correct. The answer is a recommendation under a stated coaching model.
- Do not call a generated diagram a `real rally`. Use `doubles rally position`.
- Do not promise rating improvement, guaranteed match improvement, or coaching equivalence.
- Do not use broad `pickleball drills` positioning alone. Many results are video libraries or on-court workout plans.
- Do not show prices or trial language in the primary screenshot set.
- Do not submit localized metadata or screenshots as proof of a localized product while the binary remains English-only.

## Brand and trademark decision

The current repository already states that the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Dynamic Universal Pickleball Rating and does not calculate an official rating. That disclosure should remain after rebranding because historical review artifacts and search may still surface the prior name.

The official DUPR app and `My DUPR Coach` appear in broad pickleball results. `DUPR IQ` can reasonably make a user expect rating-linked analytics or an authorized coaching product. The product instead grades generated shot choices against its own coaching principles.

Implementation gate:

- If written permission and trademark review explicitly authorize `DUPR IQ`, document the scope and permitted presentation before retaining it.
- Without that evidence, use `Pickleball IQ` before App Review.
- Do not buy ads or build press around the current name while the decision is unresolved.

## Product truth and claim boundaries

Supported by source:

- Generated legal doubles rally positions
- Four players, ball position and height, score, and rally phase
- Four plausible shot choices
- Named coaching principle and explanation after selection
- Six phases: return of serve, third shot, transition, dink rally, attacking a high ball, and defense
- Fifteen graded balls per day free
- Unlimited practice with Pro
- Phase accuracy and session history
- Ranked missed coaching principles with Pro
- Offline generated content
- Native iPhone and iPad targets

Qualify:

- Use `recommended` rather than `correct` in acquisition copy when context may support multiple valid shots.
- Call the scene a generated doubles position, not video or a real recorded rally.
- Do not imply the phase percentages are reliable weakness measurements until enough samples exist.
- `Know what to practice next` requires the fixture to contain at least five graded examples per phase so the product is not labeling an untouched phase as weak.
- Generated positions require legality tests and deterministic screenshot seeds.
